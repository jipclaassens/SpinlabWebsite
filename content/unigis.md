![](/files/2016/09/Unigis_logonw.jpg){: .side }The UNIGIS Programme is the oldest and largest distance-learning MSc programme in GIS in the world. It is offered via multiple universities worldwide under the flag of [UNIGIS International Association](http://www.unigis.net) and offers ample opportunities for professionals to acquire knowledge and skills in the field of GIS, within their existing organisation as well as to increase job mobility.

The part-time distance-learning character allows you to combine education, work and private life.

Visit [the UNIGIS website](https://vu.nl/en/education/master/geographical-information-sciences) for more information.
