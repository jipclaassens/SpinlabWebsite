SPINlab works with many partners in government and business. Some of these partnerships go back a long way.

## Geodan

This Dutch GIS consultancy, a VU start-up, is one of SPINlab's oldest and closest partners. Several people work both at SPINlab and at Geodan. The cooperation strengthens the technical capabilities of SPINlab and helps to translate research results into practice.

## Dutch Association of Real Estate Brokers (NVM)

The Department of Spatial Economics has an agreement with the NVM on the use of real estate transaction data for scientific research. In exchange, VU researchers present their findings to the NVM and share how local brokers can use the outcomes in their business. SPINlab also geocodes the NVM data, using the Dutch register of addresses and buildings (BAG) as a source.

## Netherlands Environmental Assessment Agency (PBL)

SPINlab and the Department of Spatial Economics have worked with the PBL (Planbureau voor de Leefomgeving) for many years. Researchers join PBL projects and can sometimes use PBL spatial datasets, and PBL staff take part in SPINlab research or work on their PhD.

## Rijkswaterstaat (RWS)

Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam and Rijkswaterstaat signed a framework agreement on 22 May 2014 to exchange knowledge in spatial information technologies. [Read more about this collaboration](/research/researcharchive/rws/).
