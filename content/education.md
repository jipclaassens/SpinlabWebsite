SPINlab offers many courses on GIS and spatial data analysis.

Introductory GIS courses are part of various bachelor and master programmes. Together with Utrecht University, for example, we offer the [national GI minor](https://vu.nl/en/education/minor/geo-information-minor). Students apply these skills later on in domains such as land-use change, landscape valuation and urban environmental analysis, and in their [thesis research](/supervised-theses/).

Professional, distance-learning training in GIS is available through our [UNIGIS master programme](/unigis/).
