Definite is no longer maintained and is not offered for download or sale here. This page is kept for reference.

DEFINITE (decisions on a finite set of alternatives, in Dutch BOSDA) is a decision support package developed at the Institute for Environmental Studies of Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam to improve the quality of environmental decision making. It combined several multi-criteria methods with cost-benefit and cost-effectiveness analysis, and offered extensive tools for sensitivity analysis.

The method is described in [A toolbox for multicriteria decision-making](https://www.inderscience.com/info/inarticle.php?artid=8252) (International Journal of Environmental Technology and Management, 2006).
