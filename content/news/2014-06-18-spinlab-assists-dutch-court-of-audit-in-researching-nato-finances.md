[Jasper Dekkers](https://web.archive.org/web/20141125164648/http://www.feweb.vu.nl:80/gis/organisation/staff2.asp?id=27) and [Maria Pozimski](https://www.linkedin.com/pub/maria-pozimski/83/94a/255) from SPINlab have worked together with the Dutch Court of Audit to design the first version of the court’s project website on transparency in NATO finances and helped in gathering and checking publicly available financial information. The website had to adhere to the strictest government guidelines on website design. This is a challenging limitation when the aim is to include interactive web maps that display financial information from member states. The court’s project website on NATO transparency was published last week.
