PhD researcher Vasco Diogo, who is visiting the University of Melbourne as part of his activities in the Tools for adaptation strategies project, presents his work at the Biofuel Forum in Traralgon, Victoria. He will highlight the use of a land-use modeling framework to assess the potential for biofuels. This event is organized by the Gippsland Climate Change Network and has attracted the attention of a local newspaper.
