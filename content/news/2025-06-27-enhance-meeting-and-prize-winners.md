[ENHANCE](https://research.vu.nl/en/projects/nwo-enhance-enhancing-sustainable-travel-in-small-cities-and-oute/) project partner UCL-CASA organised the 19th International Conference on Computational Urban Planning & Urban Management ([CUPUM-2025](https://cupum.co/cupum-2025-london/)). A great opportunity to also organise the ENHANCE mid-term meeting in London. So, plenty of interesting presentations and opportunities to interact with our colleagues and the larger research community on urban data analysis and modelling. With reasons to celebrate: our new postdoc Jiakun Liu won CUPUM’s best paper award, while departing postdoc Tanhua Jin had won the best paper award at the BIVEC-GIBET Transport Research Days earlier this month.
