Last January Ting Zou successfully defended her PhD dissertation at the VU University. Her thesis *Landscape service provisions and preferences on the urban–rural fringe* ‘attempts to investigate changes in landscape service supply and people’s preferences towards these services over space and time on the urban–rural fringe, and assist in providing such information’.

Ting’s promoter was Prof. Dr. H.J. Scholten and the co-promoters were Dr. E. Koomen and Prof. Dr. J.E. Vermaat. We congratulate Dr. Zou with her successful thesis defence and wish her all the best with the following steps in her career! The full thesis can be viewed and downloaded [here](https://research.vu.nl/en/publications/landscape-service-provisions-and-preferences-on-the-urbanrural-fr).
