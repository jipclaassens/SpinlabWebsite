SPINlab advises researchers of all disciplines at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam on spatial data and spatial analysis. The lab also encourages cooperation between academia and industry, and with other partners such as government organisations and secondary schools.

## Spatial data

Spatial analysis needs spatial data. [Geoplaza](https://geoplaza.vu.nl/) is the VU's gateway to a number of spatial datasets, which SPINlab distributes together with the university library. They can be viewed and in many cases downloaded there. Geoplaza also has examples of GIS projects and information on software.

If you are a VU student or employee and cannot find what you need on Geoplaza, [contact us](/contact/) to check whether SPINlab has something that matches your request.

## GIS software

Most of our courses use [QGIS](https://www.qgis.org/). We recommend the latest stable release. The VU also has a licence for ArcGIS by Esri. You can request a licence and find more on GIS software on [Geoplaza](https://geoplaza.vu.nl/en/cms/software/).

## Tools and models

We share a number of [tools and models](/support/tools/), including the Land Use Scanner.
