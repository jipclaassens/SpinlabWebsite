Spatial Knowledge graphs hold a wealth of structured knowledge about our world. To unlock their full potential, we must complement their powerful deductive reasoning capabilities with that of inductive reasoning. In my research, I try to contribute to this endeavour by investigating the applicability of deep learning on multi-modal knowledge graphs and the usefulness thereof for knowledge consumers.
