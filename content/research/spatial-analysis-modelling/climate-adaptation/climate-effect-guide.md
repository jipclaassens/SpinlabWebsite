**![Klimaat Effect Wijzer](/files/2016/07/logo_plaatjes_120_KEF.jpg)Land use-component of the Climate Effect Guide**

The VU University Amsterdam is responsible for the component **Land Use** of the ’Klimaat Effect Wijzer’ (Climate Effect Guide).

This component gives an overview of land use scenarios that can be used for climate change impact and adaptation studies.
The overview on the website contains information, documentation and advice about the use of the information. The other pieces of the puzzle on the website redirect to information about the corresponding disciplines.
