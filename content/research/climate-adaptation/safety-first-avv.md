**![Aandacht voor veiligheid](/files/2016/07/logo_avv.jpg){: .side }Safety First (AVV)**
The Safety First project (in Dutch *Aandacht voor Veiligheid*, or AVV) investigates how long-term changes in climate, land use, governance and socio-economic trends will affect flood safety in the Netherlands. The project will deliver a decision support system (DSS) that will show, using maps and images, how spatial adaptation responses can make the Netherlands climate-proof for the long term. Water expertise and spatial planning are therefore central to the study.

Start: October 2006, finished: 2008
