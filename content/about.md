The Spatial Information Laboratory (SPINlab) is the centre for research and education in geo-information science at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam. It is part of the Department of Spatial Economics in the School of Business and Economics, and is chaired by Prof. dr. Henri de Groot and Associate Prof. dr. Eric Koomen.

Our researchers come from a wide range of backgrounds. They explore new ways of collecting data, analyse spatial processes, and support decision making by developing modelling tools and digital representations of the world we live in. With student assistants, interns and PhD candidates included, the group counts about 15 to 20 people.

We work closely with partners in government and business, and we provide education and support on spatial data to students and researchers across the university.
