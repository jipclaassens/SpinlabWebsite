---
title: Privacy
description: This site sets no cookies and collects no data about its visitors.
---

This site is a set of plain pages. It sets no cookies, keeps no visitor statistics and has no forms. Embedded videos use the privacy-enhanced player of YouTube or the player of Vimeo, which load only when a page contains a video; those services have their own privacy terms.

Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam processes personal data under the [VU privacy statement](https://vu.nl/en/about-vu/more-about/privacy-statement-vrije-universiteit-amsterdam).
